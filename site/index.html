FINDINGS

What 1,000 reviews actually said

Stratified sample of 1,000 from 10,000 public Play Store reviews, 11 Aug – 16 Oct 2026. Counts are theme assignments within this sample, not shares of the Groww customer base.

BASELINE
814/ 1,000

81% carry no substantive complaint, tracking a 768/1,000 five-star skew. Reported as context, not ranked as a product issue.

TOP ISSUE THEMES
  • Technical Issues73
  • Feature Requests44
  • Poor Customer Support28
  • High Fees fee focus24

38 reviews received no theme. A review may carry up to 2 themes, so counts sum above the number of classified reviews.

IDENTIFIED FEE CONFUSION

Users aren’t disputing the price. They’re confused by buy/sell asymmetry.

19 of 25 fee-mentioning reviews are rated 3★ or below. The recurring complaint is that selling costs more than buying the same stock — read as a hidden or double charge.

BUY LEG
  • Brokerage ₹20 or 0.1%, min ₹5
  • Stamp duty 0.015% buy side only
  • STT 0.1%
  • No DP charge
SELL LEG
  • Brokerage ₹20 or 0.1%, min ₹5
  • No stamp duty
  • STT 0.1%
  • DP charge ₹3.5 + ₹16.5 sell side only

Why it happens: DP charges are levied only when shares leave the demat account, so they apply on sell transactions only — per sell transaction, not per share. It is one charge per leg, not the same charge applied twice.
